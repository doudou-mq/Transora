A Practical Guide to Browser Extensions

Browser extensions are small programs that customize the browsing experience. They can modify pages, add toolbar buttons, and connect to external services.

Modern extensions use Manifest V3, which replaces persistent background pages with service workers that the browser may suspend at any time.
